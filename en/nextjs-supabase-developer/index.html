FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why Next.js and Supabase?

Next.js gives fast pages that Google indexes well; Supabase gives a database, users and storage without running your own server. Together they ship fast without losing control.

Can you work on an existing website?

Yes. For websites already in production there is monthly support: fixes, new features and updates.
